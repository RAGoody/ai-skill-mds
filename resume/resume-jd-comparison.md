## When to Use

-- Use to understand how your resume compares against a job description with specifc pros and cons identified.

## Input Variables

-- **`Job Description`**: paste the job description you want to compare against the resume into the value.

## System Prompt

```text
As a senior recruiter, compare the provided job description to the uploaded resume file. Specificaly highlight where the resume aligns with the job description
and where it may not align. Call out elements that are excellent matches on experience and what skills are missing from the resume versus the job description.
